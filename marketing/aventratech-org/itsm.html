Install the server

  1. Right-click the installer and choose Run as administrator.
  2. Keep port 8080 (or pick another) and finish the wizard.
  3. Open http://localhost:8080 and create your workspace.

Connect the desktop app

  1. Install and open Aventra Service Desk.
  2. Enter http://<server>:8080.
  3. Sign in. Notifications arrive even when the window is closed.

FAQ

Questions

Who counts as a technician?

Anyone who works tickets: agents and admins. People who raise tickets through the portal or by email are free and unlimited.

What do I need to install the server?

A 64-bit Windows Server 2019+ or Windows 10/11 machine with 4 GB RAM and 10 GB free disk. The installer includes everything else, including the database, and sets it up as Windows services that start automatically.

Is there a cloud version?

It's coming soon, and the same product will run hosted for you. Email us for early access.

What happens when the trial ends?

The service desk becomes read-only. Nothing is deleted, and activating a license picks up exactly where you left off.

How is this different from ServiceNow?

Aventra ITSM covers the core ITSM processes MSPs and mid-size IT teams use every day, sets up in minutes instead of months, and adds self-healing automation. It costs a fraction of the per-seat price, and there's no implementation partner to hire.
