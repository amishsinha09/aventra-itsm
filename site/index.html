Tickets that close themselves

The Aventra agent watches every endpoint. When it spots a known issue, the whole ticket lifecycle happens on its own, and your technicians only see what actually needs a person.

Detect

The agent spots a problem, such as low disk space, a stopped service or a hung update.

Open

Fix

The matching self-healing playbook runs automatically, with every step logged on the ticket.

Close or escalate

Fixed issues are resolved as "auto-remediated". Anything else goes straight to the right team, flagged urgent.

Questions

Who counts as a technician?

Anyone who works tickets: agents and admins. People who raise tickets through the portal or by email are free and unlimited.

Cloud or on-prem?

Both. The cloud version runs on our infrastructure. The on-prem version is a Windows installer that sets up the service desk and its database on your own server. Your team uses either one in a browser, or in the free desktop app.

Do I need a credit card for the trial?

No. The cloud trial is 14 days of Pro, and the on-prem trial is 30 days. If you subscribe during the trial, billing starts when the trial ends.

What happens if I don't subscribe?

Your service desk becomes read-only. Nothing is deleted, and choosing a plan picks up exactly where you left off.

How is this different from ServiceNow?

Aventra ITSM covers the core ITSM processes MSPs and mid-size IT teams use every day, sets up in minutes instead of months, and adds self-healing automation. It costs a fraction of the per-seat price, and there's no implementation partner to hire.
